How bundles share the desk

1
One account, one bundleAn account is a maker or a taker in exactly one bundle, so each bundle's inventory is its own.
2
One market, one bundleTwo of your bundles never quote the same market, so they can't trade with each other.
3
Wallet limits cap everythingEach bundle has its own limits, and your wallet limits cap the total across all of them.
4
Stop all means allStop all cancels every bundle's orders. Pause stops one bundle.
