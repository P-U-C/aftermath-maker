Your own market‑making desk. Working for you.

Maker runs a desk from your own accounts. It keeps a bid and an ask resting on the markets you choose, earns the spread and maker rewards, hedges what fills, and stops itself at your limits. You set the strategy. It does the shifts, and tells you what it's doing in plain words.

While you're away.

The desk keeps working within the limits you set, and keeps a running account of why it did each thing.

Quotes both sides

A bid under the price and an ask over it, refreshed as the price moves.

Hedges what fills

Past your trigger, the taker account trades the other way so a move can't run away with you.

Stops at your limits

Exposure, inventory and drawdown caps stop it before they're crossed. One Stop ends everything.

Explains itself

Every number has a line of plain English, and Explain mode walks you through the desk.
