Watch a market being made.

Your ask rests above the price and your bid below it, in front of everyone else's orders. Takers trade with them, you earn the gap, and when too much piles up on one side your taker account hedges it. On your desk the same picture is drawn from your real quotes, fills and the live book.

1
The spread

Your bid and ask rest either side of the price. The gap between the two capsules is the spread you earn.

2
Fills

Sellers come down to hit bids and buyers come up to lift asks. When yours fills, it drops into your inventory.

3
Inventory and skew

Fills leave you holding some of the asset. Maker re-quotes both sides leaning away from it, to bring you back to flat.

4
Hedging

Past your trigger, the taker account sends a market order the other way, so a move can't run away with you.

5
Rewards

Venues pay makers for resting good quotes near the price. Time and size near the price earn a share.
