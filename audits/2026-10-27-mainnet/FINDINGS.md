# Mainnet audit findings

Audit date: 2026-10-27  
Audit owner: `0xf1dd50b692e6e74d17f4032dd67c6f4ce8165055aecddd874b7cd69dd2cba488`

This audit combines static review, local regressions, authenticated browser testing, and bounded Sui mainnet runs. It proved account creation, deposits, agent grants, repeated atomic replacement, exact-order cancellation, four externally triggered Maker fills, fill attribution, fee parsing, protective halt, a fill-driven hedge from the second account, actual post-hedge reconciliation, and reduce-only recovery of both accounts.

## Residual risks

- The successful MON hedge required a user-approved 500 bps cap after 75 bps failed. Thin markets can impose material execution cost; the product does not select or raise a safe cap for the user.
- The same-venue hedge and two-account reduce-only recovery are proven on mainnet, but they do not remove venue, basis, funding, liquidation, or gap risk.
- Withdrawal, builder approval removal, and capability revocation were not executed because the two-account audit setup is being retained. Their local regressions do not replace a mainnet trace.
- Aftermath documents no designated market-maker program or guaranteed per-market reward formula. Maker's ranking is a transparent heuristic, not a reward promise.
- Fair value uses Aftermath's protocol index plus local book. Independent venue inputs and cross-venue hedging are not implemented.
- Performance still lacks markouts, exact position-level funding attribution, gas converted to USD, and inventory-carry decomposition.
- The same-venue hedge reads at most the latest 200 account fills per reconciliation. A sustained high-fill strategy needs cursor-based ingestion before scale.
- The unauthenticated login endpoints retain one shared internal-source rate-limit bucket. This is acceptable for the current small whitelist but should move to a trusted edge identity before broad public access.
- A deliberate engine restart resets the run-equity high. Longer-horizon daily and lifetime loss controls are still required before materially increasing capital.
- The on-chain whitelist uses a vector and is appropriate for a small curated set, not an unbounded public registry.

## Access boundary

The bootstrap operator is immutable in the local store after first claim. Registry deployment confirmation requires that same wallet to be the transaction sender and the on-chain AdminCap owner. Every membership endpoint requires an operator session, and the management view only renders when the signed-in identity is the operator. In production, the audit member received HTTP 403 from `/api/operator/status` and no membership controls.

Four Maker fills are exactly attributed and one fill-driven hedge is confirmed from account 1746. Both account capabilities remain intentionally authorized for further testing.
